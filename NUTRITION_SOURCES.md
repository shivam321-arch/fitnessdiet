# FitMentor – Nutrition Research Library

Last reviewed: 19 Nov 2026. Each entry shows what FitMentor uses it for, and how strong the evidence is.

**Evidence labels**
- **Established:** official guideline, or consistent systematic-review evidence.
- **Emerging:** newer or less certain evidence, used carefully.
- **App rule:** a conservative FitMentor safety choice, not taken from a guideline.

## FitMentor calculation rules (summary)

1. **BMR:** Mifflin-St Jeor equation. **TDEE** = BMR × activity factor (1.2 to 1.725). This is an estimate, often ±10%.
2. **Fat loss:**
   - The deficit is set by the user's timeline, then capped at the smallest of: 20% of TDEE (15% for people aged 65+), 750 kcal/day, and a loss of 1% of body weight per week.
   - Calories never go below the larger of the BMR or the sex-specific floor: 1200 kcal (women) or 1500 kcal (men). (App rule, based on NICE NG246's limit on low-energy diets.)
3. **Muscle gain:** +10% of TDEE, kept between +200 and +350 kcal.
4. **Protein:**
   - Base: grams per kg of a *reference weight* (the actual weight, capped at the BMI-24.9 weight for people with obesity).
   - By goal: fat loss 1.2–1.6, health 1.0, fitness 1.2, recomposition or muscle gain 1.6 g/kg.
   - Age 65+: at least 1.1 g/kg.
   - Kidney disease: capped at 0.8 g/kg.
   - Never below 0.83 g/kg of actual weight (ICMR RDA).
5. **Fat:** 25–28% of energy (at most 30%, at least 20%). **Carbohydrate:** whatever energy remains, at least 130 g.
6. **Fibre:** 20 g per 1000 kcal, at least 25 g.
   - Salt: under 5 g. Added sugar: 25 g at most (none for people with diabetes).
   - Drinks: 30 ml per kg (1.6–3.0 L), plus 0.5 L if very active.
   - Kidney or heart conditions: follow the doctor's fluid advice.
7. **Cross-check:** 4 × protein + 4 × carbohydrate + 9 × fat must equal the calorie target, within rounding.
