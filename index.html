Why it didn’t work

Three hidden reasons diets fail.

1
You lost muscle, not just fat.

A chart copied from the internet ignores your height, weight, routine, health and the food your family actually cooks.

What to expect — honestly

Real change, at a pace your body keeps.

  1. Week 1–2Regular meal times, less bloating and fewer cravings. You stop feeling hungry and weak.
  2. Week 3–4The weekly average starts going down. A safe pace for most people is about 0.5–1 kg a week.
  3. Month 2–3Clothes fit better, energy is up, workouts feel stronger. Losing even 5–7% of body weight can improve blood sugar and other health markers.

Every body is different, so no app can promise exact kilos. FitMentor promises a plan that’s safe, made for you, and adjusted every week.

Questions

Before you start.

Do I have to give up roti and rice?

No. Your plan uses roti, rice, dal and sabzi in the right portions for your goal. Most people lose weight on food they already eat — just measured.

Do I need a gym?

No. Walking and home workouts are enough to start. If you go to a gym, your workout plan uses the equipment there.

I’m vegetarian. Will I get enough protein?

Yes. Your plan uses dal, curd, paneer, sprouts, chana and soya to reach your protein target, and tells you honestly if your choices fall short.

Do I need supplements or protein powder?

No. Plans are built from regular food. ICMR also advises against protein supplements for building body mass.

I have diabetes, thyroid or PCOS. Can I use it?

Yes, with care. Your plan follows safe general rules for your condition and is marked for your doctor or dietitian to check. FitMentor never diagnoses or replaces medical treatment.

What if my weight stops going down?

Use the weekly check-in. FitMentor looks at 2–3 weeks of trend and your food log before suggesting a small change — never panic cuts after one bad day.

Is it really free? Is my data safe?

Starting is free. Your data is saved to your account and never sold. You can delete everything anytime. Privacy policy
