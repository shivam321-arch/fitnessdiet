Privacy Policy

FitMentor · Last updated 19 November 2026

FitMentor is a diet, calorie and workout guide for Indian food. This page explains what information we keep, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and Google account ID from Google. If you use a password, we store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, never the password itself.
  • What you enter in the app: age, gender, height, weight, goals, food preferences, allergies, health conditions you choose to tell us, food logs, water, steps, workouts and body measurements.
  • Email codes: one-time codes for sign-up and password reset. These are stored only in scrambled form and are deleted after 10 minutes.

Why we use it

  • To calculate your calorie and nutrient targets and build your diet chart.
  • To save your data to your account, so it works on any phone or laptop.
  • To answer your questions with the AI mentor.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers.

Services that process your data for us

  • MongoDB Atlas: stores your account and app data.
  • Render: hosts the website and server.
  • Amazon Web Services (Bedrock): processes the questions and food descriptions or photos you send to the AI mentor.
  • Brevo: sends sign-up and password-reset emails.
  • Google: handles “Continue with Google” sign-in, if you choose it.

If you don’t create an account

Your data stays only in your browser on that device. Clearing the browser data deletes it.

Health information

Health details are optional and are used only to make your plan safer. FitMentor gives general nutrition information. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Deleting your data

In the app, open More → Settings → Delete everything. This permanently deletes your account and all saved data from our database. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

FitMentor is meant for adults. People under 18 should use it only with a parent and a doctor’s guidance.
